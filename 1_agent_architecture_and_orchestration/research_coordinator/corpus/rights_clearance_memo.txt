Rights clearance memo

Date: 2025-06-21
Source: internal legal guidance, creative services division

This memo sets out the clearance position for delivered work that involved
generative tooling at any stage of production.

Provenance must be recorded per asset, not per project. A project-level
declaration is not sufficient because clearance questions arrive about
specific frames, images or passages, and a project-level record cannot answer
them. Teams should log the tool, the model version and the prompt lineage for
each generated asset that reaches a delivery path.

Training-data exposure remains the principal unresolved risk. Where a vendor
cannot describe the provenance of its training corpus, delivered work built on
that vendor's output should be treated as carrying unquantified risk, and that
assessment should be surfaced to the client rather than absorbed silently.

Licensed and rights-cleared corpora reduce but do not eliminate this exposure,
because indemnity terms vary widely and several are capped well below the
value of a typical delivery.

Human authorship should be documented wherever a copyright claim is intended.
The safest posture is a contemporaneous record of the human contribution, kept
alongside the asset provenance log.
